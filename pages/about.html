Our Story

Rooted in Ruiru.

Destiny Garden began with a simple idea: a space where people gather, eat well and stay a little longer. Dining, celebration, rest and play share one garden, so a single visit can hold a whole day.

What guides us

Three ideas shape every table, event and corner of the garden.

Unhurried

Meals and moments are paced for lingering, never for the clock.

Together

Spaces sized for couples, families, teams and whole communities.

Welcoming

Every age is looked after, from toddlers to grandparents.
